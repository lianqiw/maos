Overview

The Multithreaded Adaptive Optics Simulator (MAOS) is an end-to-end adaptive optics (AO) simulator. It can simulate many kinds of astronomical AO systems, including single conjugate AO (SCAO), multi-conjugate AO (MCAO), laser tomography AO (LTAO), multi-object AO (MOAO), and ground layer AO (GLAO). It is written in C, runs on multi-core CPUs, and can optionally use Nvidia GPUs.

Historical benchmarks

Time per simulation step for the TMT NFIRAOS baseline (30 m aperture, 6 LGS, dual order 60x60 DMs), measured on hardware of the time:

  • Dual Intel Xeon W5590 at 3.33 GHz: 1.3 s with 8 threads.
  • Intel Core i7-2600 at 3.4 GHz: 1.9 s with 4 threads.
  • Intel Core i7-2600 at 3.4 GHz: 1.67 s with 8 hyper-threads (4 physical cores).
  • One Nvidia GTX 580 GPU: 0.2 s.
  • Eight Nvidia GTX 580 GPUs: 0.03 s.
