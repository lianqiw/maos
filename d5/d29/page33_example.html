Available configurations

The baseline configurations in config/maos are complete and can be used with -c:

  • default.conf (includes mcao_lgs.conf): LGS MCAO, the default.
  • mcao_ngs.conf – NGS MCAO.
  • scao_lgs.conf, scao_ngs.conf, scao_pywfs.conf – single conjugate AO with LGS, Shack-Hartmann NGS, or pyramid NGS WFS.
  • ltao.conf – LGS LTAO with a single DM and on axis DM fitting.
  • glao.conf – GLAO with a single DM.

The folder config/maos/examples contains telescope apertures, which are included by other files (examples/keck.conf, examples/tmt.conf, examples/gemini.conf, examples/elt.conf), and configurations of existing or planned AO systems, for example:

  • examples/keck_ngs.conf – Keck NGS AO.
  • examples/keck_kapa.conf – Keck KAPA LGS LTAO.
  • examples/strata.conf and the strata_*.conf variants: Keck STRATA with the adaptive secondary mirror (KASM) for different instruments and fields of view.
  • examples/nfiraos_lgs.conf, examples/nfiraos_lgs_noimpl.conf, examples/nfiraos_lgs_pyttf.conf, examples/nfiraos_ngs.conf – TMT NFIRAOS.
  • examples/gems_lgs.conf – a test setup of the Gemini South MCAO system GeMS.
  • examples/raven_lgs.conf, examples/raven_ngs.conf – MOAO configurations on the Gemini aperture modeled after Raven.
  • examples/morfeo_lgs.conf – LGS MCAO on the ELT aperture.
  • examples/mcao_2dm30.conf, examples/mcao_2dm60.conf, examples/mcao_3dm60.conf and the Keck variants examples/keck_2dm30.conf, examples/keck_2dm60.conf, examples/keck_3dm60.conf – wide field MCAO studies.

For example, maos -c nfiraos_lgs.conf -o nfiraos runs TMT NFIRAOS in LGS mode. Config File List shows the content of every file.
