Detailed Description

Pupil of the laser launch telescope (LLT) of a laser guide star WFS: the points, the Gaussian beam amplitude, and the uplink focus and tip/tilt/focus removal of its OPD.

Create the points of the LLT pupil: one subaperture with the same sampling as the WFS subapertures, so that the uplink OTF has the same sampling, and at least as large as the WFS subaperture. It is centered on the LLT.

Parameters
[in]lltdDiameter of the LLT in meter.
[in]dsaSize of the WFS subaperture in meter.
[in]dxSampling of the WFS subaperture in meter.
Returns
The points. Newly allocated; the caller frees it.

dmat * llt_amp ( const pts_t * pts,
real lltd,
real widthp,
real misregx,
real misregy )

Create the amplitude of the laser beam on the LLT pupil: a Gaussian whose waist (the radius where the amplitude drops to 1/e) is widthp*lltd/2, clipped by the LLT aperture of diameter lltd. The misregistration shifts the beam relative to the aperture, which clips it. The amplitude is normalized so that max(otf)=1 for the unclipped beam.

Parameters
[in]ptsThe points of the LLT pupil (llt_pts()).
[in]lltdDiameter of the LLT in meter.
[in]widthpWaist of the beam relative to lltd/2.
[in]misregxMisregistration of the beam along x in meter.
[in]misregyMisregistration of the beam along y in meter.
Returns
Amplitude on the points of pts. Newly allocated; the caller frees it.
