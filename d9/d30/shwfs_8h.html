Detailed Description

Geometry of the subapertures of a Shack-Hartmann WFS: the subaperture grid, the points in each subaperture, the lenslet fill factor, the subaperture area, the selection of subapertures, the geometric gradients, and the center of gravity (CoG) mask, noise, and gradient offset.

Create the lower left corner of the subapertures of a Shack-Hartmann WFS on a square grid centered on the aperture. A subaperture is kept if it overlaps with the annular aperture enlarged by the misregistration. Subapertures with insufficient illumination are removed later with shwfs_sa_isolated() and the area threshold.

Parameters
[in]orderNumber of subapertures across the aperture.
[in]dsaSubaperture size in meter.
[in]dinInner diameter of the aperture (central obscuration) in meter.
[in]misregMaximum misregistration of the WFS (lenslet array) in meter.
[in]misreg_aperMisregistration of the telescope aperture in meter.
Returns
The subaperture grid with spacing dsa. Newly allocated; the caller frees it.

Create the points in each subaperture. The number of points along each side of a subaperture is made even (for FFT) and the sampling is adjusted so that it divides dsa. The points are offset by half of the sampling from the lower left corner so that they are inside the subaperture.

Parameters
[in]salocLower left corner of each subaperture.
[in]dsaSubaperture size in meter.
[in]dxRequested sampling of the points in meter.
Returns
The points. Its nxsa and dx are the number of points along each side and the adjusted sampling. Newly allocated; the caller frees it.

◆ shwfs_sa_isolated()

void shwfs_sa_isolated ( pts_t * pts,
dmat * saa,
real saat )

Turn off isolated subapertures: a subaperture with area above the threshold is turned off (its area set to 0) if at most two of its eight neighbors are above the threshold. Repeated until nothing changes. Nothing is done if there are 4 or fewer subapertures.

Parameters
[in]ptsThe subapertures. Its map is created and freed.
[in,out]saaNormalized area of each subaperture.
[in]saatThreshold of the normalized area.
