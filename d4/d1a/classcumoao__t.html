Set up MOAO DM fitting in GPU for ndir directions: ray tracing operators from the tomography grid (hxp) and common DMs (hap) to each direction, and the MOAO DM operator (ha) with low rank terms and actuator slaving.

Parameters
[in]parmsSimulation parameters. Only fit.maxit and fit.cgwarm are used.
[in]moaoCPU MOAO reconstruction data.
[in]dirArray of ndir directions to fit for.
[in]_ndirNumber of directions.
[in]_gridReconstruction geometry on GPU. Kept as a reference.
