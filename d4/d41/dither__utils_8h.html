Fit i0 to sodium profile using iterative algorithm. The steps are as follows

  1. Create sub images for each sodium profile bin
  2. Fit such sub-images against i0 to determine the profile
  3. Determine subaperture tip/tilt comparing i0 and fitted i0 (using matched filter)
  4. Repeat 1-4.
Parameters
[out]sodiumThe sodium profile determined by fit (height in the first column). Reused (resized) if allocated, otherwise allocated. May be NULL.
[out]pgradThe gradients determined by fit. Allocated if NULL. May be NULL.
[out]pi0The output i0. May be NULL.
[out]pgxThe output gx. May be NULL.
[out]pgyThe output gy. May be NULL.
[in]i0iThe input i0.
[in]sepsfShort exposure PSF.
[in]dtfDetector transfer function.
[in]salocSubaperture grid.
[in]saaSubaperture area.
[in]srsaSubaperture to LLT distance.
[in]srotSubaperture to LLT clocking.
[in]siglevsSubaperture signal level.
[in]wvlwtsWavelength weights.
[in]gradoffNCPA gradient to be used for pi0,pgx,pgy output and as the initial gradient.
[in]dhThe sodium profile sampling in meters.
[in]hsLGS focusing height (m).
[in]htelTelescope height (m).
[in]zaTelescope zenith angle (radian).
[in]svdthresSVD threshold.
[in]nrepNumber of iterations.
[in]saveSave results to file.
[in]use_cacheUse cache.
