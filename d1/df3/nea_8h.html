Detailed Description

Noise equivalent angle (NEA) of WFS gradients. The NEA of the nsa subapertures is stored as nsa x 2 (xx, yy) or nsa x 3 (xx, yy, xy) matrix, or nsa x ng for ng>2 (diagonal, for raw PWFS).

Convert column vector nsax2 or nsax3 to sparse matrix. The third column of nea is coupling.

Parameters
[in]neaNEA (or its inverse or Cholesky factor) of size nsa x ng or nsa x 3.
[in]llWhether to fill the lower left (yx) coupling term.
[in]urWhether to fill the upper right (xy) coupling term.
[in]ngNumber of gradients per subaperture. If ng>2, treat as diagonal matrix (for raw PWFS).
Returns
The (nsa*ng) x (nsa*ng) sparse matrix. Newly allocated; the caller frees it.
