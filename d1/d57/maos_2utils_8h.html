dmat * wfsgrad_lltopd ( sim_t * simu,
int iwfs,
int isim,
real hs )

Compute the uplink OPD of the laser launch telescope (LLT) of an LGS WFS: the LLT NCPA, the uplink turbulence, and the tip/tilt of the uplink FSM (or the ideal FSM, which removes the tip/tilt completely and updates simu->fsmreal), jitter, and pointing errors. Used by physical optics through wfsints() and, by its tip/tilt, by geometric optics.

Parameters
[in,out]simuSimulation data. simu->lltopd is updated.
[in]iwfsIndex of the WFS.
[in]isimTime step index.
[in]hsHeight of the guide star (or of the sodium sublayer).
Returns
simu->lltopd[iwfs].
